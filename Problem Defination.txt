
Project Title: Automated Report Generation & KPI Monitoring System

Business Context: Manual preparation of weekly operational reports is time-consuming and prone to errors. This project automates the complete reporting workflow.

Input Data: Daily operational CSV data containing date, department, and sales values.

Processing Logic: Data cleaning, transformation, weekly aggregation, KPI calculations, and threshold checks.

Output: Auto-generated Excel/PDF reports with KPI summaries.

Automation: Scheduled execution with alert notifications when KPIs breach thresholds.
